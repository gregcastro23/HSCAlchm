# Deep-Fried Chicken Marsala Recipe

**Professional-grade deep-fried chicken cutlets in a rich Marsala wine sauce with mushrooms, perfect for large-scale service.**

## Recipe Details
- **Name:** Deep-Fried Chicken Marsala
- **Service Time:** 10:15 AM
- **Prep Start:** 6:30 AM
- **Total Time:** 3 hours 45 minutes
- **Serves:** 100 portions


## Master Ingredient List

### For Chicken:
- **100 pounds** chicken breasts (to be pounded to 1/4" thickness)
- **6 quarts** seasoned flour (already prepped)
- **1.5 gallons** liquid egg wash (thinned with 1 quart water)
- Deep fryer oil at 350°F

### For Tilt Skillet Marsala Sauce (Full Batch):
- **1 quart** olive oil
- **1 pound** butter (for starting)
- **25 pounds** fresh mushrooms, sliced 1/4" thick
- **3 pounds** shallots or onions, minced
- **2 cups** minced garlic (about 4 heads)
- **1.5 gallons** Marsala wine (24 cups)
- **1.5 gallons** chicken stock concentrate + water
- **2 pounds** butter (for finishing)
- **2 cups** fresh lemon juice
- **3 bunches** fresh parsley, chopped
- **Salt and black pepper** to taste

### For Cornstarch Slurry:
- **4 cups** cornstarch
- **4 cups** cold water
- **4 cups** cold stock concentrate

## Detailed Instructions

### Step 1: Chicken Prep (1 hour - 6:30-7:30 PM)
1. Set up pounding stations (4 people if available)
2. Pound chicken to 1/4" thickness, working in 10-pound batches
3. Stack between parchment paper
4. Set up dredging line:
   - Station 1: Seasoned flour (shake off old flour first)
   - Station 2: Egg wash
   - Station 3: Final flour dredge
5. Begin double-dredging as soon as first batch is pounded
6. Stage in 4" hotel pans with parchment between layers

### Step 2: Sauce Base (30 minutes - 7:30-8:00 PM)
1. **Heat & Fat (5 minutes):**
   - Set tilt skillet to 400°F
   - Add 1 quart olive oil and 1 pound butter
   - Heat until butter melts and starts to foam

2. **Mushroom Searing (20 minutes):**
   - Add 25 pounds sliced mushrooms in batches
   - **DO NOT STIR for first 5 minutes** - let them sear
   - Stir every 3-4 minutes after initial sear
   - Cook until golden brown and moisture evaporates
   - Season with 3 tablespoons salt

3. **Aromatics (5 minutes):**
   - Push mushrooms to edges of skillet
   - Reduce heat to 325°F
   - Add minced shallots/onions to center, cook 2 minutes until fragrant
   - Add minced garlic, cook 1 minute (don't let it brown)
   - Stir everything together

### Step 3: Deep Frying Chicken (1.5 hours - 8:00-9:30 PM)
1. **Fryer Setup:**
   - Temperature: 350°F
   - Batch size: 10-12 cutlets per basket
   - Fry time: 3-4 minutes per batch
   - Two fryers running = 20-24 cutlets every 4 minutes

2. **Holding Protocol:**
   - Drain each batch for 30 seconds
   - Transfer to sheet pans with rack
   - Hold in 170°F warming cabinet
   - **DO NOT sauce until all chicken is fried**

### Step 4: Complete Sauce (Parallel with Frying - 8:00-8:30 PM)
1. **Deglaze with Marsala (8 minutes):**
   - Increase tilt skillet to 375°F
   - Add 1.5 gallons Marsala wine in 3 additions
   - Let alcohol cook off between additions
   - Scrape bottom thoroughly

2. **Add Stock & Reduce (10 minutes):**
   - Add 1.5 gallons stock (concentrate + water)
   - Bring to rolling boil, then reduce to simmer
   - Cook until reduced by 1/3

3. **Prepare Slurry (2 minutes):**
   - Mix 4 cups cornstarch with 8 cups cold liquid
   - Whisk until completely smooth

4. **Thicken Sauce (5 minutes):**
   - Bring sauce back to simmer
   - Stream in slurry while whisking constantly
   - Simmer 2-3 minutes until glossy
   - Taste and adjust salt/pepper
   - Reduce heat to 180°F holding temp

### Step 5: Assembly & Finishing (30 minutes - 9:30-10:00 PM)
1. **Combine Chicken & Sauce (20 minutes):**
   - Reduce tilt skillet to 180°F
   - Add fried chicken in batches (40 pieces at a time)
   - Arrange in overlapping rows
   - Ladle sauce over each batch
   - Let rest 3-4 minutes to absorb

2. **Final Butter & Lemon (5 minutes):**
   - **TURN OFF tilt skillet**
   - Tilt forward to pool sauce
   - Add 2 pounds butter in pieces around edges
   - Rock skillet gently to emulsify
   - Drizzle 2 cups lemon juice over chicken
   - Sprinkle with chopped parsley

3. **Transfer to Hotel Pans (5 minutes):**
   - Use slotted spatula to transfer chicken
   - 12-15 cutlets per 4" hotel pan
   - Ladle 6-8 cups sauce per pan
   - Cover loosely with foil
   - Label pans 1-10 with times

### Step 6: Final Setup & Quality Check (10:00-10:15 PM)
- Verify all pans at proper temp (145°F minimum)
- Set up service line
- Reserve 2 quarts sauce in bain-marie for refreshing
- Clean tilt skillet if doing multiple batches

## Critical Timing Notes

### If Running Behind:
- Start frying before sauce is complete
- Reduce sauce more aggressively (higher heat)
- Skip the resting period in tilt skillet
- Sauce directly in hotel pans

### If Running Ahead:
- Hold fried chicken at 140°F (no higher)
- Keep sauce at 160°F
- Don't combine until 15 minutes before service
- Add butter/lemon just before serving
